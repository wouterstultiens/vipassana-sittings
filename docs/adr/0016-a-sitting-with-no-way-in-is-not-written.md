# 16. A sitting with no way in is not written

Date: 2026-09-09

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Some rows and pages announce a sitting but give no way to enter it. The Spain
row for WhatsApp named three daily times and a Sunday sitting, and gave only a
volunteer's phone number. Dhamma Adicca's three daily sittings named the times
and sent the old student to a schedule page that holds the link of each slot.

The calendar showed both. The detail panel then said "No direct link. Use the
host page." That line asks the old student to do the work the calendar exists
to do: they open a page, read a schedule again, and find out for themselves
whether they can sit at all.

## Decision

Every join gives a way in: a link, or a number to call. The schema refuses a
join with neither, so a rule that cannot be entered is never written.

When a page holds the link per slot, the way in is that page: the platform is
`website` and the url is the page. When nothing at all leads in, the rule is
left out and the host keeps its other rules, or none.

The detail panel loses the "No direct link" line, because no sitting can reach
it.

## Consequences

The Spain WhatsApp rules are gone. Their times sit close to the Teams rules of
the same host, so the host reads as one that moved to Teams.

Adicca's three daily rules now carry the schedule page as their way in. The old
student opens it, taps the slot, and reads the Google Meet link there. The
button says "Open in Host website", which is what happens.

A host whose every rule loses its way in keeps its file and its page link, and
holds no place on the calendar. `United Arab Emirates` already reads that way.
